# Najmul Hasan

Language Models · AI Alignment

[Website](https://najmulhasan-code.github.io) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=YL8xF4MAAAAJ&hl=en) · [GitHub](https://github.com/najmulhasan-code) · [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/najmulhasan-cs-math) · [X](https://x.com/_najmulhasan)

My research interests center on language models and AI alignment. I am particularly interested in the design and training of language models, including how training choices shape their capabilities and behavior, and in developing models that are more capable, reliable, and aligned.

I completed a B.S. in Computer Science, with minors in Mathematics and Physics, at the University of North Carolina at Pembroke, where I worked with [Dr. Prashanth BusiReddyGari](https://www.uncp.edu/about/directory/prashanth-busi_reddy_gari.html) and [Dr. Shaohu Zhang](https://zhangshaohu.github.io/). I was an AI Safety Research Fellow at [Algoverse](https://algoverseairesearch.org). I completed [MIT AI Alignment's AI Safety Fundamentals](https://aialignment.mit.edu/) program and [BlueDot Impact's Technical AI Safety](https://bluedot.org/courses/technical-ai-safety) course.
